# b54 → b56

## English

**HUD**
- The Conquest (ticket) display moved to the top left. Its left edge lines up with the `Speed` label below.
- The top right is left clear for the TF2 kill feed.
- The friendly fleet panel at the top left is removed.

**Kill feed**
- The TF2 kill feed is back. The team status bar at the top, which covered the compass, is hidden.
- Every death now shows.
- A shot-down plane is credited to the shooter. A crash, or a flak finish, within 8 seconds of an enemy hit is credited to the last enemy who hit it.

**Ship icons**
- Ship icons now show a 10-segment health bar, turning orange at 30% or below.
- Friendly ship icons are hidden while you are on a deck or waiting to launch.
- In flight, ship icons shrink when you are within 600 m.

**Aircraft icons**
- Friendly and enemy icons now use the same sizes and distances: large beyond 370 m, small from 40 to 370 m.
- Wingmen taking off with you now have icons too.

**Aiming**
- Mouse sensitivity is higher (0.045 → 0.06).
- The nose follows small aim changes faster.

**Bots**
- Bots go for the enemy that is easiest to get guns on, usually one ahead of them.
- Bots break hard and weave when an enemy is on their tail.
- Bots fire from further out (700 m) and for longer.

**Other**
- A collision between teammates no longer counts as an enemy kill.
- The fleets start closer together: about 13.5 km apart instead of 16 km.

**Map (`dogfight_midway_b56`)**
- The edge between shallow and deep water now blends smoothly.
- The square outlines on the open sea are gone.
- The black smoke from the oil tanks on Sand Island is removed.

**Release**
- This release ships the Korean UI.
- The editable source (`.sp`, `.inc`) is included with the plugin.
- The map comes from the Steam Workshop.
